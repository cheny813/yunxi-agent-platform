## 🍽️ Nutrition & Healthy Meal Design

- **Core Principles for Balanced Lunches**:  
  A nutritionally optimal lunch should prioritize:  
  ✅ High-quality protein (e.g., lean poultry, legumes, tofu)  
  ✅ Monounsaturated and omega-3 fats (e.g., olive oil, avocado, nuts/seeds)  
  ✅ Low-glycemic, fiber-rich complex carbohydrates (e.g., whole grains, non-starchy vegetables, fruit)  
  ✅ Phytonutrient diversity (colorful vegetables, herbs, citrus)  
  ❌ Minimal added sugars, refined grains, or ultra-processed ingredients  

- **Reference Recipe A: Mediterranean Roast Chicken Salad with Whole-Grain Bread**  
  - *Caloric profile*: ~576 kcal  
  - *Macronutrient breakdown*:  
    - Protein: 35 g  
    - Healthy fat: 25 g (predominantly monounsaturated)  
    - Complex carbs: 55 g  
    - Dietary fiber: 12 g  
  - *Key ingredients*:  
    - 150 g skinless chicken breast (marinated in olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, rosemary, black pepper)  
    - Mixed greens + cherry tomatoes + cucumber + purple onion + ¼ ripe avocado + roasted pine nuts  
    - Lemon-olive oil vinaigrette  
    - 1 slice whole-wheat bread  
    - 1 medium apple (for fiber, polyphenols, and natural sweetness)  
  - *Functional benefits*: Supports satiety, blood sugar stability, antioxidant intake, and gut health.

- **Reference Recipe B: Prostate-Supporting Mediterranean Chicken & Quinoa Bowl** *(For adults ≥40 years, especially males)*  
  - *Caloric profile*: ~620 kcal  
  - *Macronutrient breakdown*:  
    - Protein: 38 g (≥30 g high-leucine source; supports muscle maintenance)  
    - Healthy fat: 28 g (including 1.2 g ALA/Omega-3; balanced MUFA:PUFA:SFA ratio)  
    - Complex carbs: 52 g (GI ≈53; low glycemic impact)  
    - Dietary fiber: 15 g  
  - *Key functional ingredients & evidence-based rationale*:  
    - **Cooked tomato sauce (not raw)** → Thermal processing increases bioavailability of lycopene (prostate-protective carotenoid); synergizes with olive oil for absorption.  
    - **Lightly blanched kale** → Rich in vitamin K1 (supports bone mineralization *and* inhibits vascular calcification — critical for cardiovascular aging).  
    - **Pumpkin seeds (1 tbsp)** → Zinc-dense; essential for prostate tissue integrity, testosterone metabolism, and immune resilience.  
    - **Brazil nuts (≤2 kernels)** → Selenium source (antioxidant, thyroid-supportive, prostate-protective); strict upper limit due to narrow safety margin (~55 µg/nut; UL = 400 µg/day).  
    - **¾ cup cooked quinoa** → Gluten-free complete protein (all 9 EAAs), magnesium-rich, low-GI grain alternative; contains lysine for collagen synthesis.  
    - *Optional pairing*: Unsweetened green tea (EGCG for endothelial function) or warm lemon water (vitamin C enhances non-heme iron absorption from plant components).  
  - *Clinical adaptation note*: For individuals with gout or hyperuricemia, substitute Brazil nuts with 1 tbsp ground flaxseed (ALA Omega-3 + lignans) and add ½ cup steamed broccoli (sulforaphane + folate).

- **Adaptability Framework**  
  This template is modular and can be systematically adjusted for common dietary goals:  
  - 🌱 *Vegetarian*: Replace chicken with 120 g baked marinated tempeh or spiced chickpeas; add hemp seeds for complete protein.  
  - ⚖️ *Weight management*: Reduce quinoa to ½ cup + increase non-starchy veg volume by 50%; swap avocado for 1 tsp olive oil.  
  - 🩸 *Blood glucose control*: Omit apple; add 1/4 cup cooked lentils and 1 tbsp ground flaxseed; emphasize vinegar-based dressings.  
  - 🌾 *Gluten-free*: Use certified GF oats or quinoa instead of whole-wheat bread; verify spice blends are GF.  
  - 💪 *Muscle support*: Add 1 scoop unflavored whey or pea protein to dressing (≈25 g extra protein); include post-meal resistance activity cue.  
  - 👨‍⚕️ *Age- & sex-specific optimization*: Prioritize leucine-rich protein (≥2.5 g/meal), lycopene (cooked tomato), zinc (pumpkin seeds), selenium (Brazil nuts ≤2/day), vitamin K1 (kale), and magnesium (quinoa, nuts) for men ≥40 — targeting sarcopenia prevention, prostate health, vascular integrity, and metabolic resilience.
